# Module 13: Software Risk Management

## Learning Objectives
- Identify and categorize risks (technical, project, business).
- Perform risk analysis (probability, impact, exposure).
- Apply risk response strategies (avoid, mitigate, transfer, accept).
- Monitor risks throughout the project lifecycle.

## Key Topics

### 1. Risk Management Framework
- **Risk** = Uncertainty × Impact.
- **Process** (Boehm):
  1. Risk identification
  2. Risk analysis
  3. Risk planning
  4. Risk monitoring
  5. Risk control

### 2. Risk Identification
- **Categories**:
  - Technical (performance, reliability, security).
  - Project (resources, schedule, budget).
  - Business (market, legal, competitive).
- **Techniques**:
  - Brainstorming, Delphi, checklists, SWOT analysis.
  - WBS-based, scenario analysis.

### 3. Risk Analysis
- **Qualitative**:
  - Probability (Very Low → Very High).
  - Impact (Negligible → Catastrophic).
  - Risk priority matrix.
- **Quantitative**:
  - **Risk Exposure (RE)** = Probability × Loss.
  - Decision trees, Monte Carlo simulation.

### 4. Risk Response Strategies
- **Avoidance**: Change plan to eliminate risk.
- **Mitigation**: Reduce probability or impact (e.g., prototypes, training).
- **Transfer**: Outsource, insurance, warranties.
- **Acceptance**: Contingency plan, reserves.

### 5. Risk Monitoring & Control
- **Risk register** (updated regularly).
- **Risk metrics**: Number of active risks, risk burndown.
- **Reviews**: Weekly risk review meetings.

### 6. Tools & Techniques
- **PERT** (Program Evaluation Review Technique) for schedule risk.
- **Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA)**.
- **Risk management software**: Jira Risk Manager, Active Risk Manager.

## Learning Activities
- Create a risk register for a project (e.g., developing a mobile banking app).
- Simulate a risk review meeting: prioritize and assign owners.

## Assessment
- Risk management plan (4 pages: identification, analysis, response, monitoring).
- Quantitative risk analysis mini-case.

## References
